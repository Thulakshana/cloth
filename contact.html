Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer personal styling services?

Yes! Our experienced style consultants offer complimentary personal styling appointments. Schedule your session by calling +1 (555) 123-4568 or selecting "General Inquiry" in the form above.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unworn items with original tags. All sale items are final sale. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to select international destinations. Shipping rates and delivery times vary by location. Contact us for specific information about shipping to your country.

Can I schedule a private shopping appointment?

Absolutely! We offer private shopping experiences by appointment. Enjoy personalized attention, complimentary refreshments, and exclusive access to our collection. Contact us to schedule your appointment.

Do you offer alterations?

Yes, we provide professional alteration services for all items purchased at Atelier Noir. Our skilled tailors ensure the perfect fit for your garments. Alterations are available at an additional cost.
